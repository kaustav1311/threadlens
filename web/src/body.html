How it works

  1. Export the chat

    In WhatsApp, open the chat → ⋮ or the contact name → More → Export chat → Without media. You get a .txt or .zip.

  2. Drop it here

    The file is read in this browser tab. Parsing handles Android and iPhone exports, 12- and 24-hour clocks, and Word copies of a chat.

  3. Pick a lens

    Debate, Personal, Work or Overview. Each lens shows the measures that matter for that kind of conversation. Copy or download the report.

What it measures

Questions vs. assertions
Share of each person's messages that ask something. People who are testing a claim ask; people defending one assert.
Absolutist words
always, never, every, nothing… A validated marker of black-and-white thinking (Al-Mosaiwi & Johnstone, 2018).
Evidence words
source, proof, data, court, report… How often someone reaches for something checkable.
Heat
A transparent 0–1 hostility heuristic from insults, profanity, group labels, put-downs and shouting, damped by message length. Watch its trend, not a single value.
Tone
Average sentiment from the open VADER lexicon (Hutto & Gilbert, 2014). English-first, so it undercounts Hinglish.
Moral vocabulary
Words tied to care, fairness, loyalty, authority and purity, after Moral Foundations Theory (Graham, Haidt & Nosek, 2009).
Rhetorical cues
Phrase patterns for whataboutism, false choices, exits and returns, unfalsifiable certainty and personal attacks. Each cue links to the messages that matched.
Rhythm
Who starts conversations after a 6-hour gap, median reply times, and after-hours messaging.

Privacy and fair use

Where does my chat go?

Nowhere. The page's code runs in your browser. The hosted version ships with a Content-Security-Policy of connect-src 'none', so it cannot send data anywhere even if it tried. You can check this in your browser's network tab, or download the single HTML file and run it offline.

Is this a psychological assessment?

No. These are word counts and phrase patterns. They cannot detect personality, intelligence or any condition, and they misread sarcasm, quotes and mixed languages. Use them to start a better conversation, not to settle one.

Should I analyse someone else's messages?

A chat you are part of is also the other person's. Keep reports private, anonymise names before sharing anything, and never publish someone's messages without their consent.

Are there limits?

Files up to 25 MB and 250,000 messages. The page also allows 8 analyses per 10 minutes, which keeps your browser responsive on large exports. There is no server, so nothing else needs rate-limiting. The optional self-hosted API has its own per-IP limits.

Can I get deeper analysis?

The repository includes a Python CLI and a self-hosted API that add machine-learning toxicity (Detoxify) and fallacy classifiers. They run on your own machine, too.
